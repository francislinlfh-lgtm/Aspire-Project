# Aspire Project V1 — Research Specification (rev. 2, post-audit)

**Aspire is an experimental platform for operationalizing psychological theories as
executable computational mechanisms and evaluating them against human behavioral
data.** The architecture is a means to that end, not the contribution.

Document map: model provenance in [MODEL_AUDIT.md](MODEL_AUDIT.md) · equivalence
analysis and the discriminating experiment in [IDENTIFIABILITY.md](IDENTIFIABILITY.md)
· the three studies in [STUDY_1A.md](STUDY_1A.md), [STUDY_1B.md](STUDY_1B.md),
[STUDY_1C.md](STUDY_1C.md) · literature positioning in
[RELATED_WORK.md](RELATED_WORK.md) · priority reading in
[READING_LIST.md](READING_LIST.md).

## 0. Binding rules (project constitution, extended)

1. Every equation carries one label: **[LIT]** literature-derived (citation required;
   page/equation verification noted if the source is not yet in hand), **[ADAPT]**
   adapted with stated justification, **[PROV]** provisional hypothesis. Untraceable
   equations are relabeled [PROV] or deleted.
2. Every empirical claim carries a citation.
3. Every model must make a prediction that could be wrong, and the document that
   introduces it must say what result would falsify it.
4. Linking assumptions (model quantity ↔ human measurement) are explicit, separable
   components — never buried in code.
5. The LLM parses and renders; it does not plan, reason, remember, update beliefs,
   infer psychology, track perspective, or decide to clarify.
6. Deterministic, seeded, replayable; fit-then-predict; pre-registered analyses.
7. The objective is not to make Aspire more impressive. It is to determine whether it
   can teach us something existing models do not already tell us.

## 1. Research questions (revised by the audit)

**RQ1 (Study 1A).** Can the explicit domain-mixture listener model ([ADAPT] from
Heller, Parisien & Stevenson, 2016) reproduce established human perspective-taking
behavior — the critical/control interference contrast (Keysar, Barr, Balin & Brauner,
2000) and its gradedness — under a fit-then-predict protocol?

**RQ2 (Study 1B).** Can an LLM parser convert natural-language instructions into the
model's structured frames without altering the cognitive result and without semantic
leakage across the boundary?

**RQ3 (Study 1C).** Does the explicit model plus audited boundary reproduce the human
behavioral signatures better than end-to-end LLM listeners on identical trials — and
do LLM listeners behave as if they possess any stable perspective weight at all?

**RQ4 (open; not a V1 claim).** What decision process governs clarification? Inputs
hypothesized (interpretation uncertainty, cost of misunderstanding, cost of
clarification); no mathematical form committed until the literature review justifies
one. Interface stub only.

**Explicitly out of reach of V1 (identifiability):** adjudicating serial adjustment vs
simultaneous weighing. Final-choice data cannot separate them
(IDENTIFIABILITY.md, Prop. 1); that adjudication is deferred to experiment D1
(IDENTIFIABILITY.md §4), which requires process-level dependent variables and a
faithful process-level formalization of the serial account that does not yet exist.

## 2. The model set (post-audit)

| Model | Status | Role in V1 |
|---|---|---|
| P-MIX(w) | [ADAPT] — Heller et al. (2016); our simplifications confessed in MODEL_AUDIT §1.3 | primary quantitative account; `w` is the psychological payload |
| P-EGO | reference limit (w=0) | manipulation check; already falsified as a complete account |
| P-CG | reference limit (w=1) | manipulation check; already falsified as a complete account |
| P-ANCHOR(p) | [PROV], reclassified by audit | **not a competing model** — retained in code solely as the identifiability exhibit (choice-equivalent to P-MIX; proven in tests) |
| response rule (sampling) | [PROV] linking assumption | must be fitted/sensitivity-analyzed in 1A across matching / softmax / argmax+lapse |

State variables (unchanged, seven items): object percepts with visibility flags,
derived mutually-visible set, instruction frame, posterior π, entropy H(π), one policy
parameter, RNG seed.

## 3. The paradigm

The director task (Keysar et al., 2000): closed, enumerable referent space; perspective
ground-truth computable from the display; two decades of human data; a live theoretical
dispute; LLM comparators can run identical trials. Listener-only: the human paradigm's
director was scripted, so no speaker policy is modeled (deferred to the
grounding/entrainment version). Known infidelity to fix before fitting: our superlative
scalar semantics vs the paradigm's vague positives ("small") — MODEL_AUDIT §3.3.

## 4. The three studies (kept separate; never blurred into one claim)

- **[Study 1A](STUDY_1A.md) — cognitive model validation.** Fit-then-predict against
  human data; parameter recovery before any fitting; sensitivity over response rules.
  No LLM anywhere.
- **[Study 1B](STUDY_1B.md) — language-boundary validation.** Parser accuracy,
  downstream invariance (scripted vs parsed frames, same seeds), leakage batteries,
  round-trip audit. Engineering claim only.
- **[Study 1C](STUDY_1C.md) — architecture comparison.** DV-matched comparison against
  LLM listeners; implied-w analysis (the cognitive model as a measurement device on
  the LLM). Contribution bounded by prior LLM director-task work (RELATED_WORK.md §8).

Sequencing: 1A → 1B → 1C. D1 (the discriminating experiment) follows 1A if resources
allow; it is an extension, not part of the minimal V1 claim.

## 5. Research gap (canonical statement)

See IDENTIFIABILITY.md §6 for the full conditional statement. Short form: the
competing accounts of perspective use have never been implemented against a common
task interface with explicit, separable linking assumptions, formally derived
equivalence classes, and fit-then-predict comparison on shared data — with LLM
listeners runnable in the same harness. Banned phrasings anywhere in the project:
"first," "novel architecture," "psychologically realistic AI," "human-like cognition."

## 6. Implementation status

Unchanged from rev. 1 (package `cogsim/`, no LLM inside, 7 software tests passing —
which validate specification conformance, not science; MODEL_AUDIT §2). Upgrades
required before any fit, recorded and deliberately not yet built: graded within-domain
distributions, explicit response-rule component, dataset-matched stimulus builder
(MODEL_AUDIT §4).

## 7. Roadmap

| Version | Content | Gate to next |

## 8. V1 success criteria

1. Study 1A: recovery-validated fit reproduces the interference contrast and
   gradedness on held-out conditions; boundary models fail on the same data.
2. Study 1B: downstream invariance within pre-registered threshold; zero accepted
   leakage events; failure taxonomy reported.
3. Study 1C: pre-registered comparison completed and reported *whatever the outcome*,
   including LLM wins.
4. Every equation in every document carries its label; every claim its citation; the
   verification-pending items (Heller et al. equations; dataset availability;
   flagged citations) are resolved before submission.
